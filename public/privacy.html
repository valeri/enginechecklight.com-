Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

EngineCheckLight (enginechecklight.com) helps drivers understand car problems. This page explains what we collect and why. We keep as little as we can.

What we collect

  • What you send for a diagnosis: car make, model, year and engine, error codes, the sound you chose, your description and any photo. We send this to our AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the answer. Photos are not stored by us after the answer is produced.
  • Your email, if you sign in or join the Pro waitlist. We use it only to send sign-in links and, if you joined the waitlist, to tell you when Pro launches.
  • Diagnosis history, if you are signed in: the last 50 results, so you can see them on any device. Without signing in, history stays only in your browser.
  • Usage counters: a random device cookie and your IP address are used to count free checks per day and prevent abuse. Counters are kept for a short time only.

Cookies

We use two functional cookies: one for your sign-in session and one random device ID for the daily free-check limit. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Who we share with

Anthropic (AI answers), Netlify (hosting and storage) and Resend (sign-in emails). We do not sell your data.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To delete your account, history or waitlist entry, email us and we will delete it.

Not professional advice

Answers are AI estimates. Always confirm with a qualified mechanic before major repairs, and never drive a car that may be unsafe.

Contact

Email: privacy@enginechecklight.com
